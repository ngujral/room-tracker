# App store listing (draft)

Store search ranks mostly on the **app name/title**, then the **subtitle / short description** and
(Apple) the **keywords field**, then downloads, ratings and reviews. So the key search words —
TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, contribution room — go in the title itself.

**Full description** (limit 4,000):

> Know exactly how much TFSA, RRSP and FHSA room you have left — and never pay a CRA over-contribution penalty again.
>
> CRA My Account is often months behind because banks report your contributions late. Room Tracker keeps a live count: enter your starting room once, log each deposit, and see what's left instantly.
>
> **Built for Canadian households**
> • TFSA, RRSP and FHSA room for you and your spouse, side by side
> • Works across every institution: TD, RBC, BMO, Scotiabank, CIBC, National Bank, Questrade, Wealthsimple, iA and more
> • Over-contribution warnings before CRA's 1%-a-month penalty applies
> • TFSA withdrawals shown as room coming back on January 1
> • RRSP contributions counted against the right tax year, including the first-60-days rule
> • FHSA annual and $40,000 lifetime room
>
> **Where should it go?**
> A simple guide to which account suits each investment — like holding US dividend ETFs in your RRSP to avoid the 15% US withholding tax.
>
> **Key dates**
> Countdowns to the FHSA year-end, new TFSA room, the RRSP deadline and tax filing day.
>
> **Private by design**
> No sign-up. No bank passwords. Your numbers stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere.
>
> General information only, not tax advice. Confirm your contribution room with CRA.
