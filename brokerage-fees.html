Why FX costs matter more than commissions

A $9.99 commission on a $5,000 trade is 0.2%. A 1.5% currency conversion on the same trade is $75 — and you pay it again when you convert back. If you buy US-listed stocks or ETFs, the conversion method usually matters more than which broker charges $0.

Norbert’s Gambit: converting for about 0.1%

Norbert’s Gambit converts currency by buying a stock or ETF listed in both Canadian and US dollars (commonly DLR and DLR.U), then journaling it to the other currency and selling. The cost is the bid-ask spread plus any commissions or journal fee — roughly 0.1% for conversions of a few thousand dollars or more, instead of 1.5%–2.5%. Most Canadian brokerages support it, though some need a phone call to journal the shares, and Questrade and Wealthsimple charge a $9.95 journal fee.

Other ways to cut currency costs

  • Hold USD in a USD account so dividends and sale proceeds stay in US dollars until you need them.
  • Use Canadian-listed versions of US index ETFs if you don’t want to convert at all — though in an RRSP, US-listed ETFs avoid the 15% US withholding tax on dividends.

Common questions

Which Canadian brokerage has the lowest fees?

For CAD-only investors, any $0-commission broker (Questrade, Wealthsimple, National Bank, Qtrade, BMO InvestorLine, Desjardins) costs essentially nothing. For US investing, Interactive Brokers and moomoo have the lowest currency conversion costs.

Are $0-commission trades really free?

The commission is free, but you can still pay currency conversion fees on US trades, ECN fees on some order types, and the bid-ask spread.

What is Norbert’s Gambit?

A way to convert CAD to USD (or back) by buying an inter-listed security like DLR, journaling it to the other currency and selling — usually around 0.1% instead of 1.5%–2.5%.

Which big-bank brokerage is cheapest?

BMO InvestorLine, which moved to $0 stock and ETF trades in September 2026. CIBC Investor’s Edge charges $6.95 per trade; TD, RBC and Scotia charge about $10.

How much do Canadian banks charge to convert USD?

Typically 1.5%–2.5% built into the exchange rate; independent measurements have put TD’s spread at about 2.6%–3.3%.
