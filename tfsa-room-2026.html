TFSA contribution room for 2026

The TFSA dollar limit for 2026 is $7,000. If you’ve been eligible since the TFSA began in 2009 and have never contributed, your total room is $109,000.

How your TFSA room is calculated

Your room for the year is:

  1. Every annual limit since the year you turned 18 (or 2009, whichever is later) while living in Canada,
  2. minus everything you’ve contributed,
  3. plus anything you withdrew in earlier years — withdrawals come back as room on January 1 of the next year, not right away.

That last rule is the one that catches people: if you take $5,000 out in June and put it back in August, that August deposit uses new room. If you had no room left, it’s an over-contribution.

Why CRA My Account can show the wrong number

Banks and brokerages report your TFSA deposits and withdrawals to CRA after the year ends. So in November, CRA My Account usually still shows your room as of January 1 — without this year’s contributions. If you deposit based on that number, you can over-contribute without realizing it, especially with TFSAs at more than one institution.

The fix is to start from CRA’s January 1 figure and subtract every deposit you’ve made this year, at every bank. That’s exactly what Roomleft does.

What happens if you over-contribute

CRA charges a tax of 1% a month on the highest excess amount for every month it stays in the account. Withdrawing the excess stops the clock, but remember the withdrawal itself doesn’t create new room until next January.

What to hold in a TFSA

Growth investments suit a TFSA best, since gains are never taxed. US-listed dividend ETFs are a weaker fit: the 15% US withholding tax on dividends is lost in a TFSA, while it’s waived in an RRSP under the Canada–US tax treaty.

Common questions

How much TFSA room do I have in 2026?

Add up every annual limit since you turned 18 (from 2009 onward), subtract your contributions, and add back withdrawals from previous years. If you’ve been eligible since 2009 and never contributed, it’s $109,000.

What is the TFSA limit for 2026?

$7,000 — the same as in 2024 and 2025.

When do TFSA withdrawals give room back?

On January 1 of the following year. Re-contributing a withdrawal in the same year uses new room.

Why doesn’t CRA show this year’s contributions?

Institutions report TFSA activity to CRA after year-end, so CRA My Account typically lags until the following spring.

What is the TFSA over-contribution penalty?

1% a month on the highest excess amount, for each month the excess stays in your TFSA.
